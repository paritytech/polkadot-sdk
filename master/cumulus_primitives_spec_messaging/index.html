Primitives for the Speculative Messaging protocol.

A sender parachain keeps each outgoing stream as an MMR and commits all stream roots as one StreamsRoot (a keyed trie). Messages travel between collators; the relay chain only checks that each receiver’s Requires matches a sender’s Provides.

The relay-visible types (StreamsRoot, RequiresSet) are in polkadot-primitives. This crate has the parachain-side types and the off-chain wire format.

Structs§

StreamsRoot
The sender’s per-block stream commitment root. Defined relay-side and re-exported here. Root of a sender’s stream commitment tree: a compact binary trie keyed by the 8-byte StreamId encoding, with the streams’ MMR roots as leaves.

ROOT_TAG
Stream root: H(ROOT_TAG ++ leaf_count[8, LE] ++ bag(peaks)[32]) (mmr::MmrRoot::from_bag). Binding the count stops a peer from presenting the same peaks at a forged position. The empty MMR has no peaks to bag; its root is the count-0 case, with a zero bag (mmr::empty_root).
