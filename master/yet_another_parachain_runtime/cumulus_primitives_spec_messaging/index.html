Primitives for the Speculative Messaging protocol.

A sender parachain keeps each outgoing stream as an MMR and commits all stream roots as one StreamsRoot (a keyed trie). Messages travel between collators; the relay chain only checks that each receiver’s Requires matches a sender’s Provides.

The relay-visible types (StreamsRoot, RequiresSet) are in polkadot-primitives. This crate has the parachain-side types and the off-chain wire format.

An append-only MMR extension proof: O(log n) connecting nodes that, with a frontier’s own peaks, reconstruct a newer root. The mmr_lib gen_ancestry_proof form of the design’s connecting_nodes. Verification computes the new root; it is never declared alongside the proof. It holds node hashes only, no message payloads.

MessagesResponse
Response: payloads from base on, plus the proofs binding them and everything before them to the requested StreamsRoot. Nothing is trusted; fabricated peaks, payloads or proofs cannot reproduce a committed root.
MessagingInherentData
The messaging inherent: this block’s consumption, per (source, stream). A block that consumed nothing carries none.
MmrFrontier
A peaks-only MMR frontier: the O(log n) state a stream continues from, and the accumulator that continues it. leaf_count fixes where an extension proof’s connecting nodes go.
MmrInclusionProof
A single-leaf MMR inclusion proof: mmr_lib’s [MerkleProof] items (the sibling path plus the other peaks) and the node count it was generated against. Used by the event and register head read. Where MMRExtensionProof moves a frontier forward, this places one leaf under a fixed MMR. The root is derived, never declared.
MmrRoot
A stream’s committed root at some point in its history: the leaf count bound over the bagged peaks. A newtype so it cannot be confused with a StreamsRoot, the commitment-tree root.

PrivateKind
A private-use kind byte.
Register
The receiver’s whole channel state as the sender sees it. Frozen core. Published as the ack-stream’s lossy, latest-wins leaf payload and read out-of-band by the sender via an inclusion proof. The first publish is the channel acceptance.
RegisterRead
A register as read: the peer’s latest Register and its position on the peer’s Ack stream. Kept together so no register can be stored without its position.
RequiresLift
One lift, carried in the PoV. Matched by position to a source’s StreamId-sorted record streams; a mispaired lift cannot verify because tree_proof binds the record’s key.
StreamProof
A membership proof that (stream, stream_root) is committed under a StreamsRoot. Served by the sender, verified by the receiver. Carries no leaf index; the key drives the walk.
StreamsRoot
Root of a sender’s stream commitment tree: a compact binary trie keyed by the 8-byte StreamId encoding, with the streams’ MMR roots as leaves.
WindowGrant
Advisory send-window credit a receiver grants a sender beyond the confirmed watermark. Not enforced: registers are lossy and read late, and on an ordered stream the receiver cannot reject without stalling. The hard bound is the sender STF’s message-size constant. A grant may shrink between publishes.

Functions§

build_requires
Synthesize the candidate’s RequiresSet from the per-block records, in bundle order, and the PoV lifts. Sources must match exactly; a recorded source without lifts, or lifts for an unrecorded source, invalidates the candidate.
build_requires_entry
Lift one source’s streams to a single committed StreamsRoot: stitch each stream’s intervals into an endpoint, extend it to the stream’s current root, walk the tree to the StreamsRoot, and require all of the source’s streams to reach the same root. streams iterates in StreamId order; lifts matches it by position.
leaf_hash
Hash a payload into a stream MMR leaf: H(LEAF_TAG ++ leaf_version ++ payload). Source, stream and position are structural and not in the preimage. Versions are hash-disjoint, so only the correct one reproduces a committed root.
stitch
Stitch one stream’s intervals, in bundle order, into its endpoint frontier. advances must hold exactly one extension per gap (a start that is not the previous end’s root), in gap order. Gaps are proven forward, so within a candidate verified states cannot regress; across candidates that is the STF’s to hold.
verify_event
Verify an EventResponse against its EventRequest: req.at selects head (None) or leaf k, so a head response is never accepted for a positional request or vice versa. On Ok, resp.payload is verified. The public entry for event reads.
verify_exchange
Verify a /spec-msg/exchange response against its request. The variants must match (VariantMismatch otherwise); then dispatches to verify_messages or verify_event. The top-level public entry.
verify_messages
Verify a MessagesResponse against its MessagesRequest: resp.base == req.start, total payload bytes within req.max_bytes (0 means proofs only), then the payloads under req.stream / req.under. On Ok, resp.payloads are verified. The public entry for the fetch protocol.
