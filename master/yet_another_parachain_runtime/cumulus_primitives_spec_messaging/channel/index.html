The channel layer: the formats both ends exchange, and each side’s local channel state. The pallet owns the lifecycle and flow-control logic.

§Formats (consensus surface)

  • ChannelMessage is the SCALE payload of every channel-stream MMR leaf, inside crate::mmr::leaf_hash’s framing.
  • Register is the SCALE payload of every ack-stream leaf: the receiver’s complete voice in the channel, lossy and latest-wins, read out-of-band by the sender.

ChannelMessage::Hello (index 0), ChannelMessage::CloseChannel (index 1) and the Register / RegisterStatus layout are the frozen core: they parse at every channel version. Variants beyond it (such as ChannelMessage::Data at index 2) are interpreted under the session’s negotiated channel version. The tests pin their byte layouts.

§Local state

Distinct ID types per direction and kind (OutChannelId, InChannelId, BroadcastId) prevent an outbound channel ID from indexing inbound state, and let the runtime API and inherent maps fix each entry’s discipline by type.

Helper convention: a bare StreamId is one of this chain’s own streams (a key in its own commitment tree and sender-side storage). A foreign stream is returned with its owner, (ParaId, StreamId).
