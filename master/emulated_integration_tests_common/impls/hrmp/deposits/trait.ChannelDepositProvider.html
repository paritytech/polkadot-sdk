Expand description

Holds HRMP channel deposits.

A provider either applies a request within the call or accepts it and reports the outcome later through Self::OnDepositSet. While a request for one side of a channel is pending, it refuses another request for that side.

fn weight_of_try_set_deposit() -> Weight

Upper bound of what Self::try_set_deposit spends on top of what the calls’ benchmarks measure with the in-pallet provider, e.g. sending a message.
