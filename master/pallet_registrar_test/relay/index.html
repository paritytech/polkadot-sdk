The relay chain: the real paras stack plus pallet-registrar-relay and enough XCM to talk to the parachain.

The paras/configuration/shared/origin/paras_registrar half is lifted from polkadot/runtime/common/src/paras_registrar/mock.rs; the XCM half from polkadot/xcm/xcm-simulator/example/src/relay_chain. paras_registrar being the real pallet is the point: it is what proves a registration driven from the parachain actually onboards a para and takes no deposit here.
