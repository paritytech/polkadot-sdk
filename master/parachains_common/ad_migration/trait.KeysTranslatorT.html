fn gen_authority_discovery(aura: &Self::AuraId) -> AuthorityDiscoveryId

Generate an authority_discovery key.

The default implementation generates a per-validator placeholder derived from the validator’s aura raw bytes.

Why a hashed placeholder rather than zero or aura-raw:

  • for sr25519::Public the all-zero key is the Ristretto identity element, which schnorrkel accepts and against which any Schnorr signature is trivially forgeable (s·B == R collapses when P == 0). A zero placeholder is also shared by every validator across every adopting chain until they each rotate, letting any attacker squat the entire authority-discovery mesh.
  • Reusing the aura raw bytes directly would tie the authority discovery placeholder to the validator’s aura secret, giving an unwanted cross-protocol key reuse for the duration of the rotation grace period.

The hashed derivation is unique per validator and unforgeable.
